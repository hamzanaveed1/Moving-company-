# MovingPal: first 10 posts for every platform

Ten posts, each with a design (in `3-posts/`) and a caption written for LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram. Suggested rhythm: one post every two days, starting with the launch post.

**Before you post**
- Instagram does not make links clickable in captions: put www.movingpal.co.uk/get-a-quote/ in your bio and keep "link in our bio".
- Add your website and email (customersupport@movingpal.co.uk) to each profile's contact details.
- Everything here only claims what the website already promises. Do not add numbers, years, awards, insurance cover or reviews unless they are true and you can prove them.
- Each design has alt text below: paste it into the image's alt-text box (LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram all have one).

## Post 5: Packing tip  (Day 9)

Files: `05-packing-tip.png` in `linkedin-1200x627`, `facebook-1080x1080` and `instagram-1080x1350`.

**Alt text:** A stack of cardboard moving boxes beside the text "Pack room by room. Label every box."

### LinkedIn
```
A packing tip that saves hours on moving day: pack room by room and label every box on two sides with the room it belongs to and a short note of what is inside.

The crew can then place boxes straight into the right rooms, and you can find the kettle first.

A few more habits that help: put heavy items in small boxes and light items in large ones, and keep a clearly marked "first night" box with essentials.

More guidance in our moving guides: www.movingpal.co.uk/blog/

#MovingTips #Packing #Moving #Removals

## Post 9: Choosing a removal company  (Day 17)

Files: `09-choosing.png` in `linkedin-1200x627`, `facebook-1080x1080` and `instagram-1080x1350`.

**Alt text:** Four questions to ask every removal company: is the price fixed and written, what is included, how is furniture protected, who is my contact on the day.

### LinkedIn
```
Comparing removal companies? Ask each one the same four questions:

1. Is this a fixed, written price?
2. What exactly is included?
3. How will my furniture be protected?
4. Who is my contact on the day?

Also check what insurance covers your belongings in transit and be cautious of quotes given without asking about your inventory or access, or of large cash deposits.
