A move feels overwhelming mostly because there are so many small jobs. Break them into weeks and they become manageable. Use this plan as a template and adjust it to your timeline. If you have less time, jump to the section for your timeframe and catch up on anything marked "essential".

Prefer a version you can tick off? Use our [interactive moving checklist](/moving-checklist/), which saves your progress in your browser.

## 8 weeks before

- **Set your budget** and include removals, parking, packing materials, cleaning and a small buffer. Our [cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-moving-house-cost-uk/) shows what to expect.
- **Start decluttering.** Go room by room and sort into keep, sell, donate and recycle.
- **Research removal companies** and request written quotes. See [how to choose a removal company](/blog/how-to-choose-a-removal-company-uk/).
- **Check your date.** Fridays, month-end and school holidays book out first, so reserve early.
- **Read your tenancy or contract** for notice periods, deposit rules and inventory requirements.

## 6 weeks before

- **Book your removal company** and confirm the booking in writing, including date, price, crew and what is included.
- **Book packing help** if you want it. See our [packing service](/services/packing-service/).
- **Order packing materials.** Collect strong boxes, tape, paper and wardrobe cartons.
- **Notify your landlord or letting agent** if you are renting, and agree the check-out date.
- **Check parking and access.** Find out whether you need a council parking suspension and how much notice is required. Book any building lifts.

## 4 weeks before

- **Give notice** to broadband and other providers. Broadband installations can take time, so order at the new address now.
- **Plan your utilities.** Arrange to take final meter readings on moving day and set up gas, electricity and water at your new home.
- **Start packing** rarely used items such as books, off-season clothes and decorations.
- **Arrange time off work** for moving day and the day after.
- **Plan for children and pets.** Arrange childcare or pet care for the day.
- **Check your contents insurance** starts on the day you take possession of the new home, and that your belongings are covered in transit.

## 2 weeks before

- **Redirect your mail.** Royal Mail offers a redirection service. Set it up in good time.
- **Update your address** with banks, credit cards, pension providers, insurers, your employer, HMRC and the DVLA. You are legally required to update your driving licence and vehicle registration details.
- **Update the electoral register** and let your council know for council tax at both addresses.
- **Update TV Licensing** and any subscriptions or memberships.
- **Register with a new GP and dentist** if you are moving out of area, and arrange any repeat prescriptions.
- **School places.** If you are moving to a new area with children, contact the new local authority's admissions team early.
- **Confirm timings** with your removal company, solicitor or letting agent.

## 1 week before

- **Pack everything except essentials.** Label boxes by room and add a short contents list. Mark fragile boxes clearly.
- **Defrost the freezer** and plan to use up food.
- **Arrange to disconnect appliances** such as washing machines, and drain and secure them.
- **Prepare an essentials box** with kettle, mugs, tea and coffee, toiletries, bedding, chargers, basic tools and medication.
- **Collect keys** and arrange who will hand them over at each end.
- **Check the route and parking** at both ends. Put the council parking notices up if you have a suspension.
- **Confirm with your crew** who is meeting them, where the van will park, and your contact numbers.

## Moving day: before the crew arrives

- **Take final meter readings** and photos for your records.
- **Keep valuables, documents, medication and chargers with you.** Do not put them on the van.
- **Strip beds** and pack bedding in a clearly marked bag for the first night.
- **Take photos** of the empty property and any existing marks for your own records.
- **Be available to guide the crew** and answer questions about what goes where.

## Moving day: after the van is loaded

- **Do a last walk-through** of every room, cupboard, loft and garage.
- **Leave keys** as agreed and lock up.
- **Share a drop-off plan** at the new home: label rooms so boxes go in the right place.
- **Check your delivery** against your inventory and note any damage before the crew leaves. Report issues straight away.
- **Reconnect essentials first:** beds, kettle and wifi.

## The first week in your new home

- **Check meter readings** and confirm utilities are working.
- **Change locks** if you can, and check smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.
- **Find the stopcock and fuse box.**
- **Update remaining accounts:** online shopping, loyalty cards, pet microchip details and any apps.
- **Introduce yourself to neighbours.** It helps with parcels, parking and settling in.
- **Break down boxes** and arrange recycling.

## A simple rule for stress-free moving

Do a little each day rather than a lot at the end. An hour of decluttering or packing every evening for four weeks beats a panicked all-nighter the night before.
